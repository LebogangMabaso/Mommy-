A Letter to My Mother

Happy birthday, Mom.

I have loved you all my life — even in the seasons when you could only love me for mine. Long before I understood what that meant, I felt your heartbeat, and somehow I have carried it with me ever since. It is in the way I love, the way I show up, the way I keep coming home to you.

I felt your heartbeat before I understood my own — and I have loved you with it every day since.

Thank you for every version of yourself you have given us: the strength, the softness, the sacrifice, the shelter. Thank you for being someone I get to hug, laugh with, dress up for, and show off proudly as mine.

I see you. I love you. Today, and every single day after this one.

— Your daughter, Lebogang
