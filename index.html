A Letter to My Mother

Happy birthday, Mom.

I have loved you all my life, thirty years of it, and you have only loved me for a fraction of yours, because you were already living a whole life, a whole person, long before I ever came along and you knew me. And yet, somehow, I felt your heartbeat before I understood my own, and I have loved you with it every day since. It is in the way I love, the way I show up, the way I keep coming home to you.

I have loved you all my life. You have only loved me for a fraction of yours, and I love you more for it.

I value every sacrifice you have ever made, for me, for our family, and now for Nsika too. I see it in the way you show up for him the same way you once showed up for us, and I don't take one bit of it for granted.

Nsika will sing happy birthday to you today, probably only getting as far as "u na 4," kepha, and even if he can't always say it in words, let that be a gentle reminder: he has loved you all his life too. May you receive the warmest hugs from him today, and may his progress keep bringing you as much joy as he brings all of us.

Just in case no one's told you today: you are so loved, Mom. By me, by Nsika, by this whole family you built with your own two hands. Every little thing you've given us, we noticed. We kept it.

I hope God stays being good to you. I love you.

I see you. I love you. Today, and every single day after this one.

Your daughter, Lebogang
